Paths was a mobile puzzle game where you connected points of the same color without leaving any gray points.

<video autoplay loop muted>
  <source src="assets/posts/2016-02-06-paths/demo.mp4" type="video/mp4">
</video>

The hardest part was generating random levels that were still solvable. Brute force worked for small grids. Larger grids needed heuristics, and I eventually found an approach that produced levels of the sizes I wanted.

It was the first game I published on the app store on Android. I had a lot of fun playing it and some of the puzzles were very challenging.

I experimented a lot with the menus to find an appealing and simple design.
