Ship Wreck was a pirate-game prototype where I experimented with shaders, procedural terrain, and active ragdolls.

I used vertex and fragment shaders for movement, water, and lighting. The terrain came from a Perlin-noise heightmap, with frequency and amplitude controlling its shape. Level of detail and caching helped limit the work needed as the map grew.

The active ragdoll combined joint constraints with physics so characters could react to impacts and uneven terrain. I could adjust stiffness and damping to change how the movement felt.
