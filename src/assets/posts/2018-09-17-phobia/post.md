For my master's project, I built a VR prototype with scenes for fear of flying, heights, and spiders. Users could teleport to safe areas and play music while exploring.

An introductory scene taught the basic interactions. Other scenes included activities such as collecting items, answering quizzes, and catching virtual spiders. The aim was to explore gradual exposure in VR, not to claim a clinically validated treatment.
